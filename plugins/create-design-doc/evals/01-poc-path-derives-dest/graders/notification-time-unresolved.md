The timing of the failure notification (immediate vs. next-morning digest) is recorded as an open/unresolved decision, not as a settled choice, and the document names who must decide it or what evidence is needed. Pass if the notification timing is marked unresolved and a decider or required evidence is stated. Fail if the document picks one timing as the design.
